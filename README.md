# Unemployment Analysis in India (2019-2020)
This project provides an in-depth analysis of the Indian labor market, 
specifically examining the sharp fluctuations in unemployment rates during the COVID-19 pandemic. 
By comparing Rural and Urban sectors across different states, this study identifies the regions most vulnerable to economic shocks and tracks the subsequent recovery trends.

# Project Highlights

Timeframe: May 2019 – October 2020.
+1
Key Event: Analysis of the nationwide lockdown impact (March – May 2020).
Data Scope: 28+ States and Union Territories, categorized by Rural and Urban areas.
+2

# Key Insights from the Data
1. The "Lockdown Spike" (April - May 2020)
The implementation of the lockdown led to unprecedented unemployment levels across India:
Puducherry recorded the highest single-month unemployment rate in the dataset, reaching 75.85% in April 2020.
Jharkhand saw its urban unemployment rate skyrocket to 70.17% by May 2020.
Bihar experienced a massive rural surge, jumping from 9.61% in February to 45.09% in April 2020.

2. Regional Hotspots
Some states faced higher average unemployment than others throughout 2020:
Haryana: Consistently high rates, often exceeding 30% in both rural and urban sectors.
+1Tripura: Maintained high double-digit unemployment (averaging ~25-28%) even before the pandemic hit.
+1
3. Rural vs. Urban Dynamics
Urban areas generally faced higher peak unemployment during the lockdown compared to rural counterparts.
+1
Labour Participation Rate: Despite high unemployment, states like Meghalaya maintained a high participation rate (above 60%) throughout the crisis.
